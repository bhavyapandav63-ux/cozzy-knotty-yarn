Meet Bhumika,
The Maker of Every Stitch.

Hi! I'm Bhumika, the creator behind Cozzy Knotty Yarn. What started with a simple crochet hook and a love for yarn has grown into a little world of handmade creations.

From forever flower bouquets to adorable character keychains and custom accessories, every piece is thoughtfully handmade with patience, creativity and lots of love.

My goal is to make gifts feel personal and memorable. Every stitch carries a little piece of the love and care that goes into creating it.
