The Artisan Behind The Brand

Slow Fashion with Purpose: The Cozzy Knotty Story

"Cozzy Knotty Yarn began with a simple hook, a ball of soft yarn, and a passion for creating handmade gifts full of love. From forever flower bouquets to custom character keychains and phone cases, every piece is stitched by hand to bring joy."

Founded by Bhumika Rewani in Surat, every creation is customized to your favorite colors, initials, and characters—rejecting mass-produced items in favor of heartfelt, hand-stitched keepsakes.

Founded by Bhumika Rewani in Surat, the brand champions zero-waste principles—rejecting mass-produced synthetic garments in favor of durable, hand-stitched heirlooms built to last for seasons.
